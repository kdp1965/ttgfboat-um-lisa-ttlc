# LISA on GF180: the port of the ttsky26d chip to the Tiny Tapeout "Best Of All Time" shuttle

The RTL is the third LISA chip's, ttsky26d-um-lisa-ttlc at rc30 (commit
cb0cb58, 2026-10-27: RESPIN.md items 1-39, REV3.md), with everything it
has - the 24-bit core, the BF16 unit, the divider, the TTLC, the data cache,
the streaming / DDR fetch, the peripherals - and without Calvin.  The
target is the GF180 PDK (gf180mcuD, the 7-track 5 V library
gf180mcu_fd_sc_mcu7t5v0 run at 3.3 V) through the ttgf26c tooling:
tt-gds-action's custom_gds / precheck / viewer at that branch, the gf180mcuD
tile templates, LibreLane 3.0.x with open_pdks 54435919 (the version the
action enables).  This file is the port's log; RESPIN.md stays the
design's history.

## What changed for GF180

* **The two hand-instantiated cells.**  `lisa_dbg.v` and `debug_regs.v`
  keep the breakpoint and the static debug registers in latches gated by a
  strong AND of the write strobe: sky130's `dlrtp_1` (RESET_B, GATE, D, Q)
  and `and2_4` (A, B, X) are GF180's `latrnq_1` (RN, E, D, Q) and `and2_4`
  (A1, A2, Z).  The power pins under `USE_POWER_PINS` are the GF180 cells'
  VDD / VSS / VNW / VPW, tied to the design's VPWR / VGND nets (LibreLane's
  gf180mcuD names: the top module and the final netlist carry VPWR / VGND,
  the cells' VDD / VSS are connected to them).  The GF180 cell models
  declare those pins `inout`, and Verilator (the flow's lint, with the PDK
  models) refuses an inout driven from an `input` port, so the
  `USE_POWER_PINS` ports of tt_um_pettit_lisa, lisa_core, lisa_dbg,
  debug_regs and the RAM32 model are `inout` now.
* **The behavioural models** of the two cells for the RTL simulation
  (test/test_cells.v) and the FPGA (fpga/arty_a7/fpga_cells.v,
  latch2flop.py) follow.
* **lisa.sdc**'s register search matches GF180's `dffq` / `latq` library
  cell names as well as sky130's `dfxtp` / `dlxtp` (`*df*`, `*dl*`,
  `*lat*`).
* **src/config.json** is the GF180 template's (ttgf-verilog-template)
  with the chip's additions: lisa.sdc, AREA synthesis, the delay cells kept
  from the resizer (`dlya_*` .. `dlyd_*`: ~4 ns each, as the Tiny Galton
  and PRISM ports found), the clock period for GF180.  No
  SYNTH_EXCLUDED_CELL_FILE: GF180's `no_synth.cells` excludes only the scan
  flops.
* **harden/** is the sky130 flow less the register anchoring and the chip
  art: configure.py builds the configuration the way `tt_tool.py
  --create-user-config --gf` does (the tile from tt-support-tools'
  tech/gf180mcuD/tile_sizes.yaml and def/ - copies are here - and the
  3.3 V corners: LIB, STA_CORNERS, DEFAULT_CORNER, LIB_SYNTH / FASTEST /
  SLOWEST, VDD_PIN_VOLTAGE, which LibreLane's gf180mcuD defaults leave at
  5 V), and the Makefile names the PDK version directory: the ciel symlink
  in ~/projects/fossi pointed at a newer open_pdks (1689ac3f) whose
  LibreLane configuration this LibreLane (3.0.2) reads with an empty LIB -
  synthesis then has no cell library and the hand-instantiated cells are
  "not part of the design".
* **info.yaml**: the 4x4 tile (1440.32 x 736.96 um, the largest a GF180
  project may take; the sky130 chip was a 5x4, 856.52 x 511.36), the
  clock provisional.
* **The workflows**: gds.yaml publishes the locally hardened GDS with
  custom_gds@ttgf26c (pdk gf180mcuD), then precheck and the viewer at the
  same branch; docs.yaml the same; test.yaml unchanged (the RTL bench).

## What is not on the GF180 chip

* **The BF16 unit** (Ken, 2026-10-27: the biggest block that could go -
  114K um2 synthesized, 16% of the design, the lampFPU divider 81.5K of
  it).  `WANT_BF16` is undefined in lisa_core.v; the unit's sources
  (fops.v, lampFPU_div_top.sv, lampFPU_div.sv, lampFPU_fractDiv.sv,
  lampFPU_utils.v, lampFPU.h) and its bench (test/fops/) are not in this
  repository - they are in ttsky26d-um-lisa-ttlc, and the define is the
  switch.  The core without the define had six references to the unit
  outside its `ifdef`s (op_fops and op_fdiv_r in the second-stage and
  interrupt logic, op_fcmp_r in the carry and zero flag loads, fdiv_valid
  / fdiv_ready in the exec state's wait): an `else` branch declares those
  five as constants (no float op, the divide never waited on) and the
  fcmp arms are inside the `ifdef`.  The BF16 opcodes (tfa, taf, fmul,
  fdiv, fadd, fneg, fswap, itof, ftoi, fclr, fcmp) decode to nothing and
  execute as nops.  test_bf16 and test_bf16f are out of test/run_suite.sh's
  list; sdcc -mlisa's float library (bf16_add and the --bf16-float
  arithmetic) uses the instructions, so C programs with floats do not run
  on this chip.
* **Calvin** (the placement-blockage art of the sky130 chip).

## Verification

The chip bench (test/) with the GF180 cell models: the default suite,
TIMER3, WS2812, WSDMA, SPIM, TTLCIO, OVERRUN, I2C, MISC36, TTLC and
DDRSWEEP pass under Verilator, the default suite under icarus; the REV3
SDCC suite 28 of 28 on the chip bench and 27 of 27 on the core bench
(2026-10-27).  No RTL other than the two cell instantiations and the power
port directions changed.

## Area

GF180's 7-track cells are about three times sky130's high-density ones
(nand2_1 3.75 -> 10.98 um2, a flop 20.0 -> 63.7, a latch 15.0 -> 43.9,
and2_4 8.8 -> 37.3).  Synthesis of the whole design (AREA 1, the RAM32 as
flops): 29919 cells, 725768 um2 of standard cells, 3899 flops; the
sky130 chip's synthesis without the RAM32 was 25173 cells, 187848 um2.

Synthesized with the hierarchy kept (`make synth OVERLAY=` a JSON with
SYNTH_HIERARCHY_MODE keep; the per-module cell areas, um2, from Yosys's
stat against the tt_025C_3v30 library):

| BF16: the divider | 81.5K | 11% | lampFPU_div_top, lampFPU_div, lampFPU_fractDiv (61.4K), its helpers |

**Without the BF16 unit** (below): 24124 cells, 610760 um2, 3621 flops.

**What the tile holds.**  The 4x4 tile's core is 1433.6 x 729.1 um =
1045K um2.  On GF180 the flow's buffers, the hold fixes and CTS add
43-50% to the synthesized area (Ken's two BOAT projects: Tiny Galton
85.0K -> 121.3K, PRISM 60.4K -> 90.9K; the sky130 chip grew 28%), so the
whole design would place at about 1040-1090K um2 of standard cells: the
tile is full before routing, against the 64.6% the sky130 chip (its 5x4
tile) routed at with difficulty.  Without the BF16 unit and the TTLC
(-182K synthesized) it would still be about 75%; the RAM32 as a DFFRAM
macro trades 123.5K x 1.45 of placed flops and muxes for the macro's
footprint (below).  A LISA without BF16 and TTLC, the RAM in a macro, is
about 62% of the 4x4; with Timer 3 back 65%.
